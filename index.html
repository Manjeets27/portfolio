Vera

A B2B site for a bamboo-biopolymer packaging startup, built to convince FMCG procurement teams that a new material is ready for production.

  • Three product lines, each with its own page: rigid, flexible and returnable packaging.
  • Client, backer and certification logo walls (FDA, CIPET, RoHS) for instant trust.
  • A category grid showing where the material fits across eight FMCG categories.

Offshore recruitment · 6 markets

EuropaWork

A trust-first site for an offshore recruitment firm serving agencies and employers in the UK, US, Australia, Ireland and the Netherlands, plus a nursing pathway to Germany.

  • Separate pathways for agencies, employers and nurses, so visitors self-select in one click.
  • Candidate login and portal, apply flow and eligibility checker.
  • A six-step engagement process, six country pages and an FAQ.
  • An anti-fraud notice and data-compliance statements that reassure everyone.

Boutique homestay · Uttarakhand

Himtattva

A cinematic site for a Himalayan homestay at 7,000 ft. The brief: make people feel the mountain air before they book.

  • A ‘take a deep breath’ preloader with ambient flute audio and a hero video.
  • A live weather and air-quality widget for Hartola.
  • A guest pre-arrival check-in page and a corporate-retreat page.
  • An illustrated attractions map, gallery and eight local-ethos stories.
